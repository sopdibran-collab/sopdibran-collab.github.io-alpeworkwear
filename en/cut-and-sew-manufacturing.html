What can you manufacture for my brand?

We manufacture your designs, cut and sewn for your brand from your tech pack, visuals or references. Your labels, decoration and packaging are built in from the development stage, making this a full-package service. Projects are coordinated from Switzerland and made in our workshops in Kosovo, and we work with you in English or French.

How does a project run, from development to delivery?

Every project follows five steps. We review your documents and advise you, then make a sample that is sent to you for fitting and approval. Bulk production starts from the graded patterns. A final quality check compares every order with the approved tech sheet and sample. Finally, delivery is tracked to your warehouse.

01

Development

We review your tech pack, visuals and references, and can create visuals from your brand guidelines. We advise on fabrics, pattern making, stitch types, decoration, trims, finishing and packaging. Sizes are graded from XS to XXL, and fabric is sourced by composition, weight (g/m²) and Pantone.

02

Sampling

Your sample is made in 1–2 weeks and sent to you for fitting and approval, and changes are possible. The sample is charged, and its cost is credited against your first order.

03

Production

Sourcing, cutting from graded patterns, decoration on cut panels, sewing, thread trimming, pressing, folding and individual polybagging. Allow 4 weeks on stock fabrics and 6–8 weeks on other fabrics.

04

Quality control

Before shipping, we check decoration, measurements, grading and construction against the approved tech sheet and sample.

05

Delivery

By truck to European destinations in about 2 weeks, or by air in about 3 days. DAP with tracking to your warehouse, DDP on request, or collection in Kosovo by your own forwarder.

What is your MOQ, and why is it per colourway?

Our MOQ is per style and per colourway, with all sizes combined. It comes down to fabric rolls: each colourway uses its own rolls, from stock or dyed to Pantone. The minimum is 100 pieces per colourway on stock fabric and 300 pieces per colourway in Pantone. For very specific fabrics, it depends on the supplier.

How long do sampling and bulk production take?

Samples take 1–2 weeks. Once your sample is approved, bulk production takes 4 weeks on stock fabrics and 6–8 weeks on other fabrics. Shipping comes on top: about 2 weeks by truck to European destinations, or about 3 days by air. Your total lead time therefore depends on the fabric you choose and how you ship.

Do I need a tech pack?

A tech pack is the best starting point, but development can also start from your visuals or references. We can create visuals from your brand guidelines, and we advise on fabrics, pattern making, stitch types, decoration, trims, finishing and packaging. Sizes are graded from XS to XXL.

What decoration, trims and packaging do you offer?

Decoration: embroidery, printing, screen printing and 3D silicone. Trims: woven or damask labels, care and size labels, drawcords, eyelets, rib trims, buttons and zips. We grade sizes from XS to XXL and source fabric by composition, weight (g/m²) and Pantone. Every piece is folded and delivered in an individual polybag.

What does the quote include?

Your final quote itemises materials, trims, decoration (development fees included), sewing and packaging, with no hidden costs. The sample is charged, and its cost is credited against your first order. Shipping is either built into the unit price or quoted separately, whichever you prefer. Prices are tiered by quantity.

How do you ship: DAP, DDP or collection?

We ship by truck to European destinations in about 2 weeks, or by air in about 3 days. Choose DAP with tracking to your warehouse, DDP on request, or collection in Kosovo by your own forwarder. Under DAP, import duties and taxes are paid by the recipient; under DDP, we take care of them.
